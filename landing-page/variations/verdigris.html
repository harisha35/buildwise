Sheet 01 — The Problem

Right now, the job site runs on three notebooks and a phone.

  • ＋Attendance lives in a supervisor's exercise book — until it gets left out in the rain.
  • ＋Advances get remembered, not recorded, so the same ₹500 quietly gets "deducted" twice.
  • ＋Material spend is a stack of paper bills nobody has actually added up since March.
  • ＋Client invoices go out late because nobody can say which milestone is really finished.

Buildwise puts every site, every worker and every rupee on one ledger — so the answer to "what's due, and to whom" is a lookup, not a phone call to four different people.

Sheet 02 — What's On The Ledger

Six modules. One system per contracting business.

Everything a contractor already tracks by hand — just no longer lost in a drawer.

Projects

Track every site from kickoff to handover — client, budget, contract type and status, with the supervisors assigned to it.

Workers & Attendance

Mark present, absent, half-day or overtime for each worker on each site, every day — with a full edit trail on every change.

Payments & Ledger

Every Sunday, Buildwise totals the week's wages automatically and shows what's due, net of advances. You just hand over the money and mark it paid.

Materials & Suppliers

Log stock in, stock out and wastage per site, so you always know what's on hand and what it actually cost.

Quotations & Invoices

Quote the job before it starts, bill it in milestones as work progresses, and track exactly what each client still owes.

Dashboards & Reports

See attendance, dues, overdue invoices and material spend across every site — filtered to any date range you need.

Attendance in, payout out — automatically, every Sunday.

Sheet 04 — Everyone On Site, One Account

Four roles. The same site, seen differently by each.

Owner

All sites · Full access

Creates projects, sets wage rates, manages every user, and sees every rupee across the business.

Contractor

All sites · Full access

Same standing as the Owner, under a separate login — for a business run by more than one principal.

Supervisor

Assigned sites only

Marks attendance, records advances and pays workers — but only on the project(s) they're assigned to.

Accountant

All sites · Financials only

Sees wages, material spend and invoices across every project — no user or project management.

Sheet 05 — Under The Hood

Built like the record it's meant to be.

§1

Server-enforced access

Every role and permission is checked on the server — the app on your phone is never the source of truth.

§2

Passwords & transport

Password hashes via bcrypt/argon2; every connection runs over HTTPS.

§3

Nothing quietly disappears

Projects and workers are archived, never deleted — history stays intact for every past report.

§4

An audit trail that holds up

Every attendance edit and financial entry is logged with who made it and when.

§5

One app, any screen

A single responsive web app for desktop and mobile browsers — nothing to install from an app store.

§6

Paper trail, on demand

Quotations and invoices export as clean PDFs — no GST clutter, just the numbers your client needs.
